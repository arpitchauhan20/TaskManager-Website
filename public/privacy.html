Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 12, 2026 • Version 2.0

1. Introduction

Welcome to TaskFlow Pro ("we", "our", or "us"), hosted at https://task-manager-website-psi.vercel.app. TaskFlow Pro is an executive task management and calendar scheduling application designed to help users streamline task workflows, automate email notifications, and synchronize deadlines with Google Calendar.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we safeguard it, and your rights regarding your data.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect data strictly necessary to provide TaskFlow Pro's features:

  • Account Information: When you register an account, we collect your name, email address, and a securely salted and hashed password (via bcrypt with 12 salt rounds). We never store plaintext passwords.
  • Task Data: Task titles, descriptions, deadlines, priorities, categories, tags, and completion timestamps created in your workspace.
  • Google Account & Calendar Tokens: When you choose to connect Google Calendar, we receive OAuth 2.0 authorization tokens and your associated Google email address. All refresh tokens are stored using AES-256-GCM encryption.
  • Notification Preferences: Contact email address provided for task reminders and alerts dispatched via Resend HTTPS API.

3. Use of Google User Data & Google Calendar API

TaskFlow Pro requests access to Google APIs solely to enable automated Google Calendar event synchronization requested by the user:

  • Scope Requested: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events
  • Purpose: To create, view, update, and remove calendar events corresponding to tasks that you explicitly schedule in TaskFlow Pro.
  • Account Identification: openid and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email are used solely to associate your calendar connection with your TaskFlow Pro user account.

Google API Limited Use Disclosure:
TaskFlow Pro's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

We do not use Google user data for advertising, market research, training AI models, or any secondary purpose. We do not sell, transfer, or distribute your Google data to third parties.

4. Data Storage and Security

Security is fundamental to TaskFlow Pro:

  • Encryption in Transit: All web traffic and API calls are transmitted over modern TLS/HTTPS (Port 443).
  • Encryption at Rest: Google Calendar refresh tokens are encrypted using AES-256-GCM with distinct initialization vectors before being stored.
  • Multi-Tenant Isolation: Every task and record is strictly isolated by authenticated User ID. No user can access or view another user's tasks or calendar events.
  • No Local Plaintext Storage: Sensitive credentials and secrets are managed through hardened server-side environment variables and never exposed to the client bundle.

5. User Rights, Revocation & Data Deletion

You have full control over your personal data:

  • Disconnect Google Calendar: You can disconnect your Google Calendar at any time via the Settings modal in the app. Disconnecting immediately revokes active OAuth tokens and deletes the stored token from our database.
  • Revoke via Google: You can also revoke access at any time via your Google Account Security Settings.
  • Account & Data Deletion: To request complete deletion of your account, profile, and all associated task history, email us at arpitchauhan5586@gmail.com. All records will be purged within 48 hours.
